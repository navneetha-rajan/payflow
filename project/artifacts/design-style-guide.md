Build the landing page using a dark-first design system rooted in near-black backgrounds (#0F0F0F to #141414) with deliberate color-block sections that punch hard — no gradients, no soft transitions. The aesthetic is data-dashboard meets financial tool: clinical, confident, and built for people who think in numbers. Every section should feel like a widget or card pulled from a live control panel.

COLOR PALETTE: Primary background is deep charcoal-black (#111111). Secondary surfaces use a dark graphite (#1C1C1C) for cards and panels. The single dominant accent is a saturated electric orange (#F05A00 to #FF6B1A) — use it exactly as Strava and the crypto dashboard use it: as a full bleed section color, a stat highlight, a CTA button fill, and an active-state indicator. Never dilute it. Reserve a cobalt blue (#1652F0, lifted directly from the Coinbase reference) as a secondary accent for status indicators, "days overdue" counters, and the invoice sync connection badges. A muted sage-green (#4A5C47 range) from the trading dashboard reference should appear only in "recovered/paid" status chips and success states. Pure white (#FFFFFF) and off-white (#F0EFEA) handle all body text and large numerals on dark backgrounds. Use no pastels, no gradients, no glassmorphism.

TYPOGRAPHY: Use a single grotesque sans-serif family throughout — Inter or Söhne are ideal, but if unavailable, use DM Sans. Headlines at hero scale should be set at 96–120px, black weight (900), tight tracking at -0.03em, flush left — never centered. Think of the "337" from Strava, the "ADA $0.34" from Coinbase, and the "03:45:19" from the control panel: numbers are the heroes. Stat callouts like "$47,200 recovered" or "14 invoices overdue" should be set at 72–88px, bold, with the decimal or unit label set at 40% opacity in a smaller size beside it, exactly like the "$23,485.95" treatment in the crypto bento. Section labels and eyebrow text should be set in uppercase, 11–12px, letter-spacing 0.15em, medium weight — matching the "PANELS / SYSTEM EFFICIENCY / YOUR GOALS" treatment from the control panel. Body copy at 16px, regular weight, line-height 1.6. Never use a serif anywhere.

LAYOUT AND STRUCTURE: The page is composed of full-width horizontal bands alternating between black and orange, with no padding between them — hard cuts only. Inside each band, use a 12-column grid with 24px gutters and 80px horizontal page margins on desktop. The hero section is split 60/40: left side holds the headline and CTA, right side holds a live-looking mock dashboard widget showing an invoice recovery summary card — dark background, orange accent bar at top, three invoice rows with escalation stage indicators (Stage 1 through 5 shown as filled/unfilled dots). Below the hero, use a full-bleed orange band for a single massive stat: "Freelancers recover invoices 54% faster." White text, flush left, same scale as the Strava "337" treatment. Then transition into a bento-grid feature section on black: a 3-column asymmetric grid of feature cards at varying heights — one tall card (invoice sync), one wide card (AI email sequence with a mini email preview), one square card (escalation rules), one square card (recovery dashboard stat). Each card is #1C1C1C with a 1px border at #2A2A2A, 16px border radius, and an orange or blue accent element inside. Use the same card-density logic as the crypto bento reference: pack information tightly, let the numbers breathe, keep labels small.

VISUAL MOTIFS: Use lollipop/stem charts (directly from the Strava reference) to visualize "invoices recovered over time" — orange dots on black stems on an off-white panel. Use circular progress indicators (from the control panel reference) to show escalation stage completion — a ring that fills as reminders are sent. Use horizontal pill-shaped progress bars (from the control panel) inside the escalation rules section to show day-trigger thresholds. For the AI email sequence feature, show a stacked card motif: five email cards slightly fanned/offset, each labeled Stage 1–5, with urgency color-coding shifting from blue (friendly) to orange (firm) to a near-red orange (legal demand). No illustrations, no photography, no icons with rounded fills — use only flat geometric shapes, raw typographic elements, and data visualization components as decoration.

DASHBOARD MOCK SECTION: Dedicate a full-width section to a static but realistic-looking dashboard preview. Dark background. Top row: three stat tiles — "Total Overdue: $31,400" in white on graphite, "Invoices Flagged: 9" with a blue badge, "Recovered This Month: $18,750" with an orange accent bar. Below: a table of invoice rows with client name, amount, days overdue (shown as a blue number that gets more saturated the higher it goes), escalation stage (dot indicators), and a status chip (Pending in graphite, Partially Paid in sage, Recovered in green-white). This should look like a real product screenshot, not a mockup illustration. Use monospaced figures for all dollar amounts.

CTA AND BUTTONS: Primary CTA button is solid orange (#F05A00), full-width on mobile, 280px wide on desktop, 52px height, 0px border radius (sharp corners only — no pill shapes on primary actions), white uppercase label at 14px, 0.1em tracking. Secondary actions use a 1px white border, transparent fill, same dimensions. Hover states: orange button darkens to #C44A00 with no transition delay beyond 80ms. No drop shadows anywhere.

ANIMATION AND INTERACTION: On scroll, use a single consistent reveal mechanic: elements translate up 24px and fade from 0 to 1 opacity over 400ms with an ease-out curve. Stagger sibling elements by 80ms. The hero dashboard widget should have a looping typewriter effect cycling through escalation email subject lines ("Friendly reminder: Invoice #1042 is due", "Second notice: Payment overdue by 14 days", "Final warning before legal action") — 2s per line, 200ms fade between. The lollipop chart in the recovery section should animate on scroll entry: stems grow upward from 0 height over 600ms, dots pop in with a scale(0) to scale(1) spring at the end. No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no particle effects. Keep every animation purposeful and tied to data comprehension, not decoration.

MOOD: The overall atmosphere is a Bloomberg Terminal rebuilt by a design-obsessed fintech team. It should feel like the person using this tool is in control of money, not chasing it. Serious, fast, data-dense, and unapologetically dark. The orange is urgency and action. The blue is information and status. The black is authority and focus. Nothing should feel playful or approachable — it should feel like professional infrastructure for someone who bills $15,000 a month and has no patience for late clients.
